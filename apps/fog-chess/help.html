Analysis

Moves are ranked by how often Obscuro would play them, the % column (by eval alone when only one board fits what you've seen). The eval is Stockfish's average over the boards you could be facing, in pawns, from your side. Hover a move to see it on the board; click it to pick up the piece.

Markers and arrows

Click a dark square to leave yourself a piece marker; click again to change it. Right-click any square (or long-press on touch) to ring it in yellow, or right-drag between two squares for an arrow.
